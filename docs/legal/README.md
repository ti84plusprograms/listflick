# Legal and support

These documents describe the current beta product and its data boundaries.
Review them with qualified legal counsel before using them as final store or
commercial terms.

- [Privacy policy](privacy-policy.md)
- [Terms of use](terms-of-use.md)
- [Support and data deletion](support.md)

The mobile app bundles Privacy and Terms for immediate offline access from
Settings. `mobile/content/legalDocuments.ts` is the bundled source, and the
mobile legal test requires these checked-in policy files to match it exactly.
Update the shared revision identifier and both copies together whenever legal
content changes.

**Contact Support** opens a user-editable email to `aaryaman04@gmail.com`. If
the device cannot open email, the app offers separate actions to copy the
address or a privacy-safe diagnostic template. A dedicated support address and
stable public policy pages may replace these beta channels before store or
commercial launch.
